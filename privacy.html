In this preview

Right now the form only checks what you type and shows you a sample confirmation. It doesn’t send or save your name, email, phone number, or choices anywhere. There are no analytics or ad trackers on this preview. The company hosting the page may keep the usual server and security logs.

Before launch

Before this goes live, the campaign will say who runs the site, connect a real signup service, name the companies that handle the data, and publish a full privacy policy.

What the signup collects

Your email address; your name and phone number if you give them; what you asked about; whether you checked the email box, the text box, or both; which version of the wording you saw and when; and which link brought you here (a yard sign, a Facebook post, and so on). Giving a phone number by itself never signs you up for texts.

How it will be used

To answer what you asked for, and to send updates only the way you chose. Only campaign staff and the companies named in the final policy will have access. Before the campaign collects anything, it will publish a contact for people who want to see their information, have it deleted, or unsubscribe.

Questions

Call the campaign at 908-721-6664. A privacy email address will be added when the campaign publishes its final policy.
