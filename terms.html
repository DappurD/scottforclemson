Texting terms

Texting isn’t turned on in this preview. These are the proposed terms for campaign texts, for review before any messages go out.

On this page

  1. Optional signup
  2. Frequency and cost
  3. Stop or get help
  4. Privacy

Optional signup

You only get texts if you check the texts box on the signup form. It starts unchecked, and giving us your number without checking it doesn’t sign you up. Every message will say who it’s from. The sender name and wording still have to be confirmed with the texting provider.

Frequency and cost

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Texts are optional; you can skip them and still get everything else from the campaign.

Stop or get help

Reply STOP to any text to stop them, or HELP for help. Both have to be set up and tested with the texting service before signup goes live.

Privacy

How we handle your number is in the privacy notice. A final privacy policy, help contact, registered sender, and approved terms are needed before any texts go out.
